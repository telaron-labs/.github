Notes

Avatar reality check

GitHub renders org avatars as small rounded squares on a white ground in most surfaces. Whatever wins needs a version with a baked-in dark tile — the 44px/22px chips above preview exactly that crop.

Parent vs product

The hex-mesh stays with the fabric. The labs mark should feel like the agency, not a product — which is why every concept here is an object in space rather than a network diagram.

Typeface

All lockups are set in D-DIN Exp — the face SpaceX sets its wordmark in. Free licence, quietly aerospace, and distinct from the DM Sans used inside the fabric product.

Suggested pairing

Design lead's pick, revised: F2 (Twin Ascent) as the avatar — two concurrent launches from one pad is the whole company in one glance. M1 + D1 for copy, F5 (Mission Patch) for the README banner and stickers.
