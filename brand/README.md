# Telaron Labs — Brand

Parent-org identity for **telaron-labs**. The parent mark is product-neutral:
the fabric keeps its hex-mesh, Mokii keeps its warm Material blue — this
identity sits above both.

## The mark — "Limb Light"

An orbital sunrise: launch site on the planet's surface, trajectory
brightening from pad to vehicle, mint limb-glow on the horizon, and the
Southern Cross (flag layout) in the north-west corner as the Australian
signature.

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `telaron-labs-limb-light.svg` | Master. Scales anywhere; dark tile baked in. |
| `telaron-labs-avatar-512.png` | GitHub org avatar upload. |
| `telaron-labs-avatar-128.png` / `-32.png` | Small-size legibility references. |

## Typeface

Wordmark and display: **D-DIN Exp** (free licence — the face SpaceX sets its
wordmark in). Deliberately distinct from DM Sans used inside the fabric product.

## Copy

- Mantra: **From orbit to backyard.**
- Org bio (live): *Independent venture lab — building Telaron: the
  multi-cloud fabric, and Mokii: the pet-safety net. From orbit to
  backyard.* 🇦🇺💫

## Guardrails

- Parent mark never reuses a product's visual (no hex-mesh, no paw).
- Monochrome-capable derivatives only; the amber dot is the one permitted pop.
- A "T" monogram direction was explored and parked — too close to Telstra's
  mark to be worth the risk. See the concept board before reviving anything
  T-shaped.
